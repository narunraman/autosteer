Review and submission

What to include

  • The generator: the element's filler in filler_tools/, which computes every answer, and a solver test that solves the filled questions independently.
  • The data: the element's folders under data/ (example_seeds/<id>, generated_seeds/<id>, templates/<id>).
  • Your review: your marks on the sample and your notes from the element's Review step (data/review/<id>.json).

How to send it

Open a pull request to the autoSTEER repository on GitHub with those files. The maintainer fills the templates with fresh seeds, runs the solver test, and reads your review before merging.
